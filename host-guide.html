Before guests arrive

  1. Laptop → TV: open the game link (your GitHub Pages address) in Chrome and cast the tab or the whole screen to the TV.
  2. Click once on the TV page — that turns on sound and full screen. You should see “● Live” in the corner for a moment.
  3. iPad: open the same address + host.html in Safari. Enter the PIN (1986 unless you changed it). Tip: Share → Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.
  4. Tap Next once on the iPad and watch the TV change — if it does, you're connected.
  5. Turn the TV volume up. The sound plays from the laptop/TV, not the iPad.
  6. Split guests into two teams of five. Everyone else is the audience.

Backup plan: if the Wi-Fi dies, open birthday-feud.html on the laptop and run it from the keyboard (shortcuts are further down).

How Family Feud works (2-minute version)

1 · Face-off

One player from each team steps up. Read the question. Whoever answers first (raise a hand / slap the table) gives an answer. Reveal it if it's on the board.

The team whose answer ranks higher on the board wins the face-off and chooses to play (or pass to the other team).

2 · Play

The playing team takes turns, one player at a time, guessing answers. Every correct answer adds points to the bank.

Wrong answer = strike. Three strikes and they're out.

3 · Steal

After 3 strikes, the other team huddles and gets one guess. If it's on the board, they steal the entire bank. If not, the bank goes to the team that was playing.

If the playing team finds every answer before 3 strikes, they win the bank — no steal.

The game does the math

Bank, strikes, steals and scores all happen automatically when you press the keys below. You just read, react, and keep the energy up.

Using the iPad controls

The purple box at the top of the Play tab always tells you what to do next — just follow it.

  • Next ▶ / ◀ Back move the TV forward and back.
  • ↶ Undo takes back the last tap (as many times as you need).
  • Scores: the −10 / −1 / +1 / +10 buttons fix any score.
  • Feud boards: tap an answer to flip it. Red Strike for wrong answers. After the face-off, tap the team that plays. Steals and bank points happen automatically.
  • Lightning boards: tap answers as they're shouted; tap the team that said #1 first (+25).
  • Trivia: tap the team's pick, Reveal answer, then tap the team that earns the points.
  • Fast Money: Start the timer, tap the matching answer chip for each question, then Reveal. Switch to Player 2 when ready.
  • Jump the TV to… at the bottom of Play skips to any round.

Other tabs

  • 👥 Teams: type team names — they appear on the TV as you type.
  • ✎ Edit: change any question, answer or point value; add rounds; build a new question set.
  • ⚙ More: TV sound on/off, reset scores, switch question sets, answer key, backup, change PIN.

Rematch

At the end of the game, the iPad shows Start Rematch with new teams. That clears the scores and team names, shows “Who's playing?” on the TV, and runs the Rematch set: 3 new Feud boards, 10 new 1986 trivia questions and a new Fast Money.

12 · Fast Money (finale)

The team with the most points picks two players. Player 2 leaves the room (or wears headphones) so they can't hear.

  1. Player 1: tap ▶ Start (20 seconds). Read the 5 questions fast. For each answer, tap the closest answer chip (or type it in).
  2. Tap Reveal on each answer, one at a time, for the drama.
  3. Tap Player 2 — Player 1's answers hide on the TV. Bring Player 2 back and tap ▶ Start (25 seconds). If they repeat an answer, tap Duplicate! and say “Try again!”
  4. Tap Show Player 1, then reveal Player 2's answers.
  5. 200 points together = they win Fast Money! Confetti fires automatically.
  6. Tap Next for the winner screen and “Happy 40th, Paola Paz!” — then offer a rematch.

If something goes wrong

  • Tapped the wrong thing? ↶ Undo.
  • TV not changing? Look at the pill at the top of the iPad. “Offline — retrying” means Wi-Fi trouble; it catches up by itself once it reconnects. You can also refresh the TV page — it picks up exactly where you were.
  • No sound? Click once on the TV page; check TV volume; check ⚙ More → TV sound is ON.
  • iPad asks for the PIN again? Enter it — nothing is lost.
  • Start over? ⚙ More → Reset scores & progress (tap twice).

This guide works offline on any phone.
